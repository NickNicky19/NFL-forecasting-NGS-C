# Qualified sources and limits

The actual combined `ngs_{passing,receiving,rushing}.csv.gz` assets provide weekly 2022–2026 summaries. **Named 2024 assets are partial** (4/8/4 total passing/receiving/rushing rows) and were not used for fits.2025/2026 named assets are absent from the inspected inventory. The combined assets, not inferred URLs, supply those years. The obsolete `player_stats` release stops at 2024; current nflreadr `load_stats.R` routes to **stats_player**, whose `stats_player_week_YEAR.csv.gz` files actually support 2022–2026. Failed old-release 2025/2026 requests returned 404; they are not missing entire seasons. The current combined old `player_stats.csv` was inspected and rejected for modern labels.

SOURCE_MANIFEST.json binds all used source bytes to URLs, asset/version clocks and hashes. FIELD_CATALOG.csv gives category/field units and opportunity denominators; each run's SOURCE_COVERAGE.json records exact per-season weekly coverage, missingness and week-zero exclusions. DASHBOARD_CHECKS.json records 33 public page checks (all three kinds over 2026 W1–7 and historicalW1 examples); a 200 HTML app shell does **not** authenticate any weekly data or publication time. The NFL glossary/load/dictionary/schedule documents were read and retained privately. No restricted APIs, premium feeds, signup service, chart extraction or tracking-data inference was used.

Only raw descriptive fields are admitted in this wave: passing depth/throw-time/aggressiveness, receiving depth/separation/cushion, rushing box-rate/time-to-LOS. Percent fields remain 0–100 percentage points, distances yards and times seconds. Throw-time excludes sacks and uses attempts; receiving distances use targets; rush box/time summaries use reported rush attempts. Exposure weighting of reported weekly averages follows the dictionary; the source does not supply complete per-event tracking. We do not equate raw counts to a more restrictive modeled handoff subset. Missing metric/exposure yields unavailable value, with its own support count in C1. No missing NGS is imputed as an observed zero.

Expected completion/CPOE, expectedYAC/residual and expected rushing/residual are withheld. The 2025 Completion Probability 2.0 provenance/retro-recomputation bridge is not authenticated. Neither source-update timestamps nor identically named fields prove historical model stability. Even descriptive field stability is a documented reconstruction assumption, not authenticated version continuity. Unknown original publication/revision times remain null; seven days is a common assumed lag only. Upstream minimum-activity filtering exists, but exact per-field/year thresholds are not proven. Empirical observed minima are not promoted to universal thresholds.

All source week-zero/season totals and postseason rows are excluded. Week 8 NGS and outcomes are excluded from fitting and evaluation. NGS joins use GSIS plus season/week/historical team to exact schedule, with no guessed name joins. The initial qualification used the wrong direction for Rams abbreviation normalization; before any C fit it was corrected to the schedule's `LA`, preserving the prefit correction receipt. Final invalid/ambiguous retained NGS counts are zero. IDENTITY_CHECKS.json also records six NGSreceiving reports with no matching offensive stat row (one 2023 PITTE and five 2025 JAXWR reports); they do not define target membership. One 2025 W18 rushing report has NGSFB versus official-statRB role; per-game official stats determine target role. There are no matched-team conflicts. This is an explicit historical source role discrepancy, not current-roster substitution.

The numerical population is a **retrospective stat-row panel**, not a complete authenticated pregame roster. Physical play evidence admits valid zero targets across categories. All-zero offensive records without affirmative physical evidence stay unknown; they are not DNP or void. Players with no row are outside this panel and their count cannot be invented. Sparse players/early exits remain.2026 schedule exactly has 107 games with week counts 16/16/16/16/15/14/14.2022 has 271 supported outcome games; the canceled BUF–CIN schedule entry has no fabricated outcomes.

Public access/mirroring does not waive upstream NFL/NGS rights. This bounded study retains raw bytes privately and publishes code, aggregates and safe hashes only. No general redistribution license or full tracking-data entitlement is asserted. Original quotes, original forecasts and venue settlement are separate unavailable evidence.
