## Proposed predictive-value test: passer-mixture change × receiver targeting preference

The exact specification is [C-DYAD-MIX-VALUE-01/v2](proposal/C_DYAD_MIX_V2.json), with [scoped feasibility review](proposal/FEASIBILITY_REVIEW.json). This is implemented descriptive information awaiting a predictive test. `ProfileBuilder._qb_pairs` in `src/receiving_profiles_c02.py` already computes native passer/receiver targeting counts; the completion-decision report explicitly queued predictive inclusion. C03 fitted features and the newer closed role/depth defensive scalar do not consume these dyads.

The hypothesis is that a change in the anticipated passer mixture interacts with earlier passer-specific receiver targeting preferences. A same-window weighted average of unshrunk pair rates equals ordinary marginal target share and is therefore **redundant**. That version is excluded.

The proposed value uses eight eligible earlier same-team player appearances to estimate shrunk pair targeting rates, then compares the latest eligible complete team game's passer mixture with that history:

`I_p = sum_q (w_latest,q − w_history,q) × (T_pq + 20 × s_p) / (A_pq + 20)`.

Here `A_pq` is qualified passer attempts, `T_pq` is explicit targets to the receiver, and `s_p` is the earlier marginal target share. The union of passer identities preserves identified non-QB and unknown-passer mass. Unseen dyads use the declared marginal prior; undefined denominators remain missing. The latest-game mixture is a fixed **usage-based forecast assumption**, not authenticated starter, health or injury information. No target-game passer mix, participation or workload enters the feature. It does not measure routes, alignment, coverage or catchability.

All sources are retained, hash-bound 2024–2026 PBP and ordinary numerical facts. No new acquisition is proposed. Source vintage remains current/revised history with the accepted strict seven-day reconstruction; it does not become authenticated pregame publication evidence. Parent training-label admission, historical feature cutoffs and game-specific query cutoffs stay separately bound. Original 32 features are unchanged.
